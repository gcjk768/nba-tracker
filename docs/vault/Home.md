# NBA Tracker

Daily NBA 2026-27 tracker and champion predictor on the home NAS: collects every game from ESPN, rates teams,
simulates the season, freezes a prediction for every game, reviews every missed pick (AAR) and re-tunes itself,
then posts a spoiler-safe Telegram digest and writes the full detail to Obsidian.
